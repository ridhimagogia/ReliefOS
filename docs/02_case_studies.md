## A. Punjab floods, India, Aug-Sep 2025 (most relevant, in your PPT)

**Facts** [V]
- Floods affected Punjab from 14 Aug 2025; became severe after 25 Aug.
- Down To Earth analysed official situation reports: the first central report mentioning Punjab was 27 Aug 2025; information exchange between state and central agencies began late.
- By the 27 Aug report, 19,597 people had been evacuated to relief camps; central deployment included Army columns, 22 NDRF teams and Air Force helicopters across Punjab, Himachal and J&K.
- Press tallies vary: Tribune cites at least 40 deaths, 2.56 lakh affected, 1,044 villages; Church Times (19 Sep) cites 1,200+ villages and 60+ deaths, about 375,000 acres of farmland underwater. Use ranges and cite the date.
- Faith-based networks (Christian, Sikh, Muslim) ran relief in parallel with government and filled gaps in remote areas; many groups reported poor coordination and delayed official aid.

**Failure pattern:** parallel actors, no shared picture of who has what and who needs what.

**ReliefOS angle:** one shared layer that gurdwara/NGO kitchens, government stocks and camps all see. Show this explicitly: "non-government relief was large and uncoordinated".

## B. Kerala floods, India, Aug 2018 (and the 2019 repeat)

**Facts**
- One-sixth of Kerala's population was directly affected per state government. [V] (ReliefWeb)
- An NGO's own report describes mobilising 51 tonnes of non-food items as 5,148 kits (about Rs 3,065 each) sent by rail free of charge. Shows the scale of volunteer logistics. [V] https://reliefweb.int/report/india/humanitarian-efforts-kerala-india
- State asked overseas donors to send money, not goods, calling unsolicited material a distraction and noting verification burden. [V]
- A volunteer NGO outreach summary lists language as a major bottleneck. [V] (Pragya, 2018)
- In 2019, Kerala ran a public district-wise requirements list and collection centres on the "Kerala Rescue" site. Also, rumours about wasted relief material hurt donations. [V]
- Free rail transport for relief material was announced by the Railway Minister in 2018. [V] (Moneylife) Useful as a real transport-capacity input.

**Failure pattern:** donation volume is not the problem; information on what is needed where is. Unsolicited goods create sorting and verification work.

**ReliefOS angle:** a public "needs board" (what is needed, how much, where) so donors give what is needed; a trust trail ("see help reach the right place") answers the rumours problem.

## C. Pakistan floods, 2022 and 2025 (regional comparison)

**Facts** [V]
- 2022: 33 million people affected. IWMI survey: 58% got no early warning; 27 stakeholders and 39 ministries involved.
- Pledged funds after 2022 were not fully absorbed because projects were delayed or never submitted (per The Friday Times, 2025).
- 2025: civil society groups set up Telegram channels to coordinate relief supplies, a sign that people already improvise this exact tool.
- A Pakistani university research project proposes a coordinated map of supplies, perceived needs and logistics routes to avoid duplication, excess or shortage, which is almost your problem statement. https://www.umt.edu.pk/odr/Research1/Research-with-Impact/13.aspx

**ReliefOS angle:** shows the problem is regional and recurring, and that ad-hoc chat groups are the current "system".
